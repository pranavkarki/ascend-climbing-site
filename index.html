About[Who We Are]

Experience a new level of adventure at Ascend Climbing, a premier rock climbing gym located on Jhamsikhel Road in Lalitpur. This dynamic facility is more than just a place to train; it's a vibrant hub for the local community. Ascend Climbing features a gym, a relaxing cafe, and a lively bar, making it the perfect destination for both serious climbers and casual visitors. Challenge yourself on the impressive climbing walls, unwind with a delicious coffee, or socialize with friends over a refreshing drink. Ascend Climbing offers a unique blend of fitness, fun, and community.
